# The Brazilian Room — Venue Guide

**Location:** Tilden Regional Park, Berkeley, CA (Alameda County)
**Capacity:** 150 seated / 225 standing reception
**Setting:** Historic 1940s lodge with redwood interiors, stone terrace, and lawn framed by Tilden's eucalyptus groves. A Bay Area favorite for weddings and milestone celebrations.

## Catering Logistics
- Full commercial prep kitchen on site: 6-burner range, double convection ovens, walk-in refrigerator, dedicated dish pit.
- Load-in via rear service drive; vehicles must be moved before guest arrival.
- No open flame permitted outdoors (park fire regulations). Sterno and induction only on the terrace.
- All rentals must be removed same night; park gates lock at midnight.

## Event Rules
- Music curfew 10:00 PM (amplified sound), event end by 11:00 PM.
- Certificate of insurance required from caterer and all vendors ($2M aggregate).
- East Bay Regional Park District requires a park use addendum for guest counts over 175.

## Recommended Layouts
- **Seated dinner (up to 150):** Rounds of 8–10 in the main hall, head table on the fireplace wall, dance floor center.
- **Cocktail reception + seated dinner:** Cocktail hour on the stone terrace with passed hors d'oeuvres and two bars; guests move inside for dinner.
- **Buffet:** Double-sided buffet along the north wall keeps lines under 10 minutes for 150 guests.

## Typical Timeline (Wedding, 150 guests)
- 2:00 PM caterer load-in and kitchen setup
- 4:30 PM ceremony on lawn
- 5:00 PM cocktail hour, terrace
- 6:15 PM seated dinner service
- 8:00 PM toasts and cake
- 10:00 PM music ends, 11:00 PM guest departure, midnight load-out complete
