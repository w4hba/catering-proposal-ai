# Piedmont Community Hall — Venue Guide

**Location:** Piedmont Park, Piedmont, CA (Alameda County)
**Capacity:** 180 seated / 250 standing reception
**Setting:** Craftsman-style hall bordering Piedmont Park with exposed beam ceilings, a wraparound veranda, and a private courtyard shaded by oaks.

## Catering Logistics
- Full commercial kitchen: 8-burner range, salamander, two convection ovens, reach-in and walk-in refrigeration.
- Direct kitchen load-in from the rear parking lot — the easiest load-in of our regular venues.
- Ample free parking (90 spaces) plus street parking.
- In-house tables (rounds of 10) and folding chairs included in venue rental; upgraded rentals optional.

## Event Rules
- Alcohol service for events over 100 guests requires a City of Piedmont permit (client obtains; allow 3 weeks).
- Music curfew 10:30 PM Fri–Sat, 9:30 PM Sun–Thu.
- No certificate of insurance required from caterer (city carries umbrella policy), but licensed bartenders mandatory.

## Recommended Layouts
- **Seated dinner (up to 180):** Rounds of 10 across the main hall; veranda used for cocktail overflow.
- **Buffet:** Kitchen-adjacent buffet alcove allows continuous replenishment without crossing guest flow.
- **Courtyard ceremony + hall reception:** Ceremony under the oaks, cocktail hour on the veranda during room flip.

## Typical Timeline (Corporate Dinner, 120 guests)
- 1:00 PM load-in and kitchen prep
- 5:30 PM guest arrival, veranda reception
- 6:30 PM buffet opens
- 8:30 PM program and awards
- 10:00 PM event end, 11:00 PM load-out complete
