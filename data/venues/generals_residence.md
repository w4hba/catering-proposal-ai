# The General's Residence — Venue Guide

**Location:** Fort Mason, San Francisco, CA (San Francisco County)
**Capacity:** 130 seated / 200 standing reception
**Setting:** Restored 19th-century officer's residence on the National Park waterfront with Golden Gate Bridge views from the lawn and glassed-in sun porch.

## Catering Logistics
- **Warming kitchen only** — no on-site cooking. All hot food arrives in hot boxes; plated service requires additional transport equipment and a per-person warming surcharge (see pricing rules).
- Load-in through the garden gate on Franklin Street side; steep ramp, cart-friendly.
- Historic building: no confetti, rice, glitter, or open flame anywhere on premises; candles must be enclosed in glass.
- National Park Service requires all trash and recycling packed out by caterer.

## Event Rules
- Events must conclude by 10:00 PM; amplified music indoors only after 8:00 PM.
- Certificate of insurance required ($2M aggregate) naming the National Park Service.
- Street parking is limited; valet or shuttle strongly recommended for guest counts over 100.

## Recommended Layouts
- **Seated dinner (up to 130):** Rounds in the ballroom, bridal party at the bay-window table.
- **Cocktail reception:** Lawn and sun porch flow, two bars (lawn + parlor) to prevent crowding at the entry.
- **Stations:** Parlor, dining room, and sun porch each host one station to move guests through the house.

## Typical Timeline (Gala Reception, 180 guests)
- 3:00 PM load-in via garden gate
- 6:00 PM guest arrival, lawn reception with passed hors d'oeuvres
- 7:30 PM stations open inside
- 9:30 PM last call, 10:00 PM event end, 11:30 PM load-out complete
